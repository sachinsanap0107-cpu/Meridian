What Meridian is — and isn't

Meridian compares what you select against patterns common to everyday conditions like colds, migraines, or minor stomach bugs. It's built to help you decide your next step faster, the way you would think it through with a knowledgeable friend.

It isn't a doctor. It doesn't examine you, know your history, or account for every possibility. Any result here is a starting point for a conversation with a real clinician, never a replacement for one.

Accounts and passwords are stored securely on the backend server. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Use your username and password to sign in.
